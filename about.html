Established as a proprietorship firm in 2017, Nagar Overseas has grown to become a trusted trader and supplier of premium hair care products, including a wide range of Hair Masks, Shampoos, Conditioners, and other personal care essentials.

With a strong focus on quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we cater to salons, beauty retailers, and personal care distributors across the country. Our products are carefully sourced to meet the highest standards of effectiveness, safety, and cosmetic appeal.
